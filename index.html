SVG Animation Showcase!

1. Hover Transition

Uses transform: scale() and fill: to transition color and scale on hover. Uses transition: transform ease and transition: fill ease to make the transitions smoothe.

2. Spinning Loader

Uses stroke-dasharray to make the spinner circle into a partial arc. Uses @keyframes and transform: rotate() to rotate the spinner once, and animation: linear infinite to make the rotation continuous.

3. Self-Drawing Line

Uses stroke-dasharray and stroke-dashoffset in a @keyframe to draw the line at page load, and fills it forwards to keep it visible after animating.

4. Fade-up Entrance

Uses opacity and transform: translateX() translateY() in one @keyframe to fade and move the lightning bolt on page load.

Reflection:
The self drawing line was by far the hardest to work with. It took me a ton of trial and error, but I got it working in the end (and I think it turned out pretty good). I had to look up what the numbers do in stroke-dasharray. It turns out it works just like Illustrator's custom stroke, so it was pretty easy to make a fun pattern for the spinner. Amazon Prime Video has hover transitions on their watchlist and info icons. Dropbox has an infinite loading spinner. The Svgator homepage has several cool example a self drawing lines. The Apple website has a bunch of fade up animations on the Apple Watch product page.
